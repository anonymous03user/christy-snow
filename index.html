Who she is

Two callings. One voice.

Christy Snow has been standing in front of rooms since 1989 — first in the bars and coffeehouses of Charlotte, where Creative Loafing voted her one of the city’s best female vocalists again and again; later in sanctuaries, retreat circles, and citywide interfaith gatherings. Somewhere along the way, the two stages became one.

An ordained minister in the Science of Mind tradition, she co-pastored the Spiritual Living Center of Charlotte for a decade before founding Awaken Spiritual Community in 2018, where she serves as spiritual director of a congregation that gathers online and in person. She has carried the same message — authenticity, equality, love — into seven years of interfaith leadership — including board service — with Mecklenburg Ministries, four years chairing Charlotte’s citywide Interfaith Thanksgiving service, and advocacy alongside the Human Rights Campaign, Equality NC, and the Campaign for Southern Equality.

And always, there is the music: fourteen albums of acoustic folk, Native-style flute, chant, and meditation — self-taught on guitar and Native-style flute, with a voice that draws comparisons to k.d. lang, Natalie Merchant, and Tracy Chapman. Whether the room is a vineyard, a sanctuary, or a retreat circle in the foothills, the work is the same: helping people remember they are free.
